# ig-caption

(Adapted from github.com/Jakeschincariol/instagram-agent-skill, MIT.)

Always show the caption the way the feed shows it: the first 125 characters in a box, everything else behind the tap. Read that box before anything else you wrote.

## First, decide which job this caption has

**Job A: the video already hooked them.** A Reel carries its own hook in the first two seconds. The caption is not a second hook. Its job is the ask, the context that makes the ask make sense, and the words people search.

**Job B: the caption is the content.** A photo, a single image, a carousel cover. Here line one is the hook: concrete, short, cut off at a cliff rather than mid-clause.

Ask which one you are writing. If the user has a Reel with a strong hook, write A and say why.

## The shape

```
Line 1      125 characters of visible space. Job A: the ask, plainly.
            Job B: the hook.
            Never a greeting, never a hashtag, never an emoji as the first character.
Body        short paragraphs, one blank line between each. Two to six.
            This is where the search terms live.
The ask     one. Comment a keyword, save it, or DM. One.
Hashtags    up to five, on their own line at the bottom, or none.
```

Limit is 2,200 characters and almost nothing needs it. A caption that earns the tap and delivers 600 characters beats one that delivers 1,800.

## Hashtags, honestly

Hashtags are labels, not a reach lever. Instagram capped hashtags at five per post in December 2025. So: up to five, specific, as topic labels. `#viral`, `#fyp`, `#explorepage`, `#foryou` describe nothing. Cut them.

## Search terms matter more than hashtags

Instagram search reads the caption text. The phrase the user wants to be found for goes in the caption as a phrase a human would type, in a normal sentence. Ask for two or three of those terms and check each appears naturally.

## Rules

- **No link in the caption.** Captions are not clickable. Bio or DM.
- **One ask.** Two asks is the same as none.
- **The keyword ask needs a keyword people can type.** One word, no spaces, no emoji, and say it out loud in the video too.
- **Write the first comment separately** if there is a link.
- **Emoji as punctuation, not decoration.** Flag anything over 4 per 100 characters.
- **Alt text is worth 20 seconds.** For carousels and photos, write it.

## The loop

1. Decide Job A or Job B and say which.
2. Draft it.
3. Humanize it: cut em dashes, slop words and anything written-sounding. Captions are short, so slop is louder here.
4. Lint it yourself: visible line within 125 chars, one ask, max 5 hashtags, no link in body, no emoji first, search terms present. Fix every FAIL; decide on every WARN out loud.
5. Print the copy-ready block, then the receipt:

```
CAPTION READY
job:        A - the reel carries the hook
visible:    118 of 125 characters used before the cut
ask:        one, comment CONTRACT
hashtags:   3
search:     "client proposals" in line 3, "agency pricing" in line 5
linter:     READY
```

Nothing is posted. The user pastes it. Write in the language the user writes in.
